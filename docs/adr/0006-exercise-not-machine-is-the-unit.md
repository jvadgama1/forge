# The exercise, not the machine, is the unit of history

History, records, suggested weights and coaching are per exercise, regardless of gym, even though a lat pulldown at two gyms may load differently. A gym is only a list of available exercises used to filter the picker. The user chose simplicity over per-machine precision and may revisit it.

## Consequences

- Every workout records its gym anyway, so a later switch to per-gym equipment can re-split history without data loss.
