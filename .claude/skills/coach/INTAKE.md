# The intake

The first coaching session starts here, when the coach notes have no `profile.md`. The intake builds the coach profile (`GLOSSARY.md`: _Coach profile_); then the coaching session goes straight on to an opening read of the whole history.

## Ask

Three rounds of questions at most, a few questions per round, in the persona's voice. Before asking, skim the data, so you ask about what it can't tell you and can say what it already shows (how long they've logged, how often, which gyms).

Cover each of these:

1. **Goals**: what they want from training and by when, in their words. Ask why each one matters to them.
2. **Age**, and anything about their health that changes how they should train.
3. **Experience**: how long they've trained, what kinds of training, the best they've done.
4. **Injuries and movements to avoid**, past and present. A current pain gets the remit's answer: what to avoid and to see a professional.
5. **Target days per week**, and how long a workout can be.
6. **Life constraints**: work, sleep, family, travel, equipment, anything that decides when and how they can train.
7. **How they want to be pushed**: anything that's off limits.

Done when every item has an answer or the user's "skip".

## Write

Show the profile in full, then write it to `profile.md` in the coach notes, with any changes the user asks for:

```markdown
# Coach profile

Updated YYYY-MM-DD.

## Goals

## About them

Age, health, experience.

## Injuries and movements to avoid

## Training time

Target days per week; how long a workout can be.

## Life constraints

## How to push them
```

Write their answers in their own terms; leave out what they skipped.

Done when `profile.md` holds the profile as the user approved it. Then go back to the skill's step 4: your opening read covers the whole history, and your challenges can start with whether their goals are the right ones.
